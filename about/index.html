THE SERIES

WHO SAID THE ROBOTS WERE THE BAD GUYS?

Human Reserve is an original, English-first, four-panel satirical comic series set in a world where artificial intelligence is running civilization—and humans have become a protected species.

The robots are not necessarily cruel. They are courteous, efficient and deeply committed to human welfare. They simply believe that humanity will be much safer if it stops making decisions.

“THE ROBOTS THINK THEY'VE SAVED HUMANITY. HUMANITY ISN'T SO SURE.”

Each episode begins as a short comic, usually with one setup, one conflict and a quietly unsettling punchline. Limited-animation versions will follow when they are ready.
