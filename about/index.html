THE SERIES

WHO SAID THE ROBOTS WERE THE BAD GUYS?

Human Reserve is an original satirical comedy series set in a future where artificial intelligence runs civilization—and humanity has become a protected species.

The robots aren't evil. They're polite, efficient, and genuinely convinced they're doing what's best for humanity. They provide food, shelter, healthcare, entertainment, and carefully managed environments.

There's just one small problem: humans are no longer allowed to make their own decisions.

At the center of the story is the Carter family, navigating daily life in a world where every human need has been anticipated, every risk has been minimized, and almost every freedom has been quietly taken away.

Through dark humor, absurd bureaucracy, and the strange relationship between humans and machines, Human Reserve explores freedom, power, human nature, and the contradictions of civilization.

After all, humans once built zoos to protect animals. Now the machines have built something remarkably similar for humans.

“THE ROBOTS THINK THEY'VE SAVED HUMANITY. HUMANITY ISN'T SO SURE.”

COMICS, ANIMATION & BEYOND

Human Reserve is an evolving story world told through satirical comics, interconnected episodes, and planned 2D animated adaptations.

Each episode explores a different absurdity of life under machine management—from happiness and human rights to work, relationships, competition, and the curious behavior of humanity itself.

Some stories stand alone. Others reveal a larger picture of how humans created a civilization they could no longer control.
